# Incubator Business Development

Stage 1 website foundation for Incubator Business Development.

## Included
- Responsive one-page business website
- Premium charcoal, champagne and bronze visual direction
- Brand mark and favicon
- Business strategy, planning, development and growth service sections
- Three introductory service packages
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Enquiry form that prepares an email in the visitor's email application

## Preview
Enable GitHub Pages from **Settings → Pages**, select the `main` branch and `/(root)`, then save. The published site will be available at the GitHub Pages URL shown by GitHub.

## Before launch
Please confirm:
- The final business name and brand identity
- Whether the package names, prices and inclusions are approved
- The correct enquiry email address (currently shown as `hello@incubator.co.za`)
- Final contact details and any required legal/privacy wording

The enquiry form currently opens the visitor's email application; it does not store or transmit submissions to a server.
